## Description
What if the Moon vanished tonight? No boom, no explosion… just a quiet disaster that grows for millions of years. 🌙❌

Pip and Ink delete the Moon and follow what happens: pitch-dark nights, lost dung beetles, corals that miss the biggest party in the ocean, shrinking tides, a deep ocean that stops being stirred, and an Earth that could wobble until it lies on its side. Plus: why eclipses are a perfect coincidence, the "Moon shield" myth, and the twist: the Moon really is leaving us, 3.8 cm every year!
