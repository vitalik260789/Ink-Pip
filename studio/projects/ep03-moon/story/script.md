# Ink & Pip — Episode 03 sample (≤1 min): "What If the Moon Disappeared?"
PIP: If the Moon vanished tonight, what would you notice first?
INK: Nothing. And that's the scary part.
PIP: Nothing? It's the Moon!
INK: For a few nights, you'd just see darker skies. Way more stars, way fewer werewolves.
PIP: Ha! So the Moon is just a giant night-light?
INK: Wait for the beach. You see, the Moon's gravity pulls the oceans into two bulges, and Earth spins right through them. That's the tide.
PIP: And without it?
INK: The Sun makes tides too, but they're less than half as strong. Crabs, birds and fish that live by the tide would be in big trouble.
PIP: Poor crabs. Anything bigger?
INK: Much bigger. The Moon keeps Earth's tilt steady, like a hand holding a spinning top. That tilt gives us our seasons.
PIP: And with no hand?
INK: Over millions of years, the top could wobble. Some models say the tilt could swing from almost zero to eighty-five degrees.
PIP: Eighty-five? That's Earth lying on its side!
INK: Half a year of daylight, half a year of night. Seasons from a horror movie.
PIP: So the Moon isn't a night-light.
INK: Nope. It's a seatbelt.
PIP: Then let's keep it buckled.
